Glowbeat app icon concepts

Three directions for the macOS app icon, drawn on the Sonoma and Tahoe icon squircle (superellipse, roughly 22.4% continuous corner radius) with a top-lit sheen, a bottom-weighted inner shadow, and a hairline top edge. Every icon is shown at 512, 128, and 32 px on a light and a dark dock, plus its 18 px menu bar template.

Colors are pulled from the app's own palettes in Packages/Effects/Sources/Effects/Palette.swift.

01Bulb + waveform

The product in one shape: a bulb whose filament is the music, so the icon says "your light is the song" before anyone reads a word. Blacklight violet ground makes the Party rainbow filament glow like a real emitter.

Export masters, 1024 px

Each master is viewBox="0 0 1024 1024" with the artwork inset to the 824 x 824 macOS icon shape and 100 px of transparent padding on every side, which is the geometry an asset catalog expects for the 512pt @2x slot. Use the copy button to grab a standalone SVG (defs included) that you can save as a .svg and rasterize into Assets.xcassets.
