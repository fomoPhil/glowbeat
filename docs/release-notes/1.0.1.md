A small fix release. Glowbeat will offer it to you, or grab the new dmg here: **[Download Glowbeat.dmg](https://github.com/fomoPhil/glowbeat/releases/latest/download/Glowbeat.dmg)**

- Two copies of Glowbeat open at once (one in Applications, one still on the disk image) used to fight over the bulbs, and one would stop seeing them. Now the second copy brings the first one forward and closes.
- Picking a color in the menu bar keeps the brightness slider where you left it. It used to jump to the Colors brightness.
- The Colors swatches are a little shorter, so more of the list fits without scrolling.
